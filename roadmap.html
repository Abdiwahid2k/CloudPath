A beginner-friendly learning plan

  1. Networking: learn IP addresses, DNS, ports, and routing.
  2. Linux: practice files, permissions, processes, and shell commands.
  3. Cloud foundations: learn compute, storage, identity, and virtual networking.
  4. Hands-on practice: create a small cloud lab and document it.
  5. Career preparation: build a portfolio and practice explaining troubleshooting decisions.

Go at a steady pace. Understanding why each technology matters is more valuable than memorizing a long list of services.
